(a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

A square of side a+b, cut into four pieces whose areas add up to the whole.

a² + ab + ab + b²
= a² + 2ab + b²

(a−b)² = a² − 2ab + b²

A square of side a, cut at a−b across and down, giving regions A, B, C and D.

⊕ not in the notes

The notes draw this square and stop. Here is the algebra it is pointing at.

A = a² − B − C − D
= a² − b(a−b) − b(a−b) − b²
= a² − 2ab + b²

a² − b² = (a+b)(a−b)

A square of side a, with a bottom strip of height b split into B and A.

Drag A off the square, then drag C so it sits above B.

⊕ not in the notes

The notes draw this cut and stop. Here is the rearrangement it is pointing at.

A = b²
C + B = a² − b²
C + B → (a+b) × (a−b)

a² + b² = c²

A square of side a+b holding a tilted square of side c and four right-angled triangles.

Drag each triangle into a corner.

4 × ½ × a × b + c²
= (a+b)²
⊕ not in the notes

The notes stop after those two lines. Here are the last two.

2ab + c² = a² + 2ab + b²
c² = a² + b²
