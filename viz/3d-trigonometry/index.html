The angle between a line and a plane

Shine a light straight down on the line. Its shadow lands on the base, and the angle you want is the one between the line and its own shadow. Tap two corners to pick a different line.

This browser cannot draw the solid. The card below does the same work flat on the page.

Drag to turn it, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap a corner to pick it.

The line is AR.
Drop a perpendicular from R to the base. It lands at C, so the angle there is a right angle.
The shadow of the line is AC, and the three points make a right-angled triangle.
The angle sits where the line meets the base: θ = 24.1°
Fold that triangle down flat and you have an ordinary two-dimensional problem.

tan θ = height ÷ base diagonal
