<h2>Method fingerprint</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Common base first, always</strong> — 9→3², 16 and 6→2 and 3, 8→2³, 27→3³, 64 and 16→4³.</li>
<li>Constant factors split off the index: 2^(4x+1) → 2^(4x)×2.</li>
<li>Once bases match, <strong>equate indices</strong> to get a linear equation.</li>
<li>Disguised quadratics by <strong>explicit substitution</strong>: &#39;Let 3ˣ be y&#39;.</li>
