The notes' one instruction: how to sketch

The sketch on shows the convention the notes want reproduced:

  • point AAA circled, with a North arrow drawn at AAA — the point you measure from;
  • CCC up-left, AC=8 kmAC = 8\text{ km}AC=8 km, angle from N to ACACAC marked 19∘19^\circ19∘;
  • BBB up-right, AB=11 kmAB = 11\text{ km}AB=11 km, angle from N to ABABAB marked 41∘41^\circ41∘;
  • a long arc sweeping round AAA labelled 341∘341^\circ341∘.

341∘341^\circ341∘ is the bearing of CCC from AAA: measured clockwise from north, the full way round rather than 19∘19^\circ19∘ anticlockwise. The notes write only the number — the relationship 341=360−19341 = 360 - 19341=360−19 is not written on the sheet.

The bearing of BBB from AAA is 041∘041^\circ041∘ — three figures, leading zero.

The notes do not carry this into a sine/cosine rule calculation, although the two distances and the included angle 19∘+41∘=60∘19^\circ+41^\circ = 60^\circ19∘+41∘=60∘ would allow one.

Method fingerprint

  • The starred instruction is 'how to sketch!' — the sketch is the skill.
  • North line drawn at the point being measured from.
  • Three-figure bearings including reflex values — 341° is shown with an anticlockwise arc.
  • Two legs from one point with distances and angles from north (8 km / 19°, 11 km / 41°).
