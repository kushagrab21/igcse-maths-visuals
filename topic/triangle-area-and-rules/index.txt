<h2>Method fingerprint</h2>
<ul>
<li>Formulas are <strong>derived, not quoted</strong> — the only fully worked derivation in the 18 photos is the cosine rule.</li>
<li>Area: from A = ½bh with h = a sin C.</li>
<li>Sine rule: set up from a shared height as sin A = h/b, sin B = h/a — <strong>the rule itself is never written</strong>.</li>
<li>Cosine rule: drop a perpendicular, split base into x and c−x, substitute x = b cos A <strong>last</strong>.</li>
<li>Applied to find an <strong>obtuse</strong> angle, negative cosine accepted: cos B = −11/24.</li>
