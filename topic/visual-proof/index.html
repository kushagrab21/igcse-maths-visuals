The one complete argument

4×12×a×b+c2=(a+b)24\times\tfrac12\times a\times b + c^2 = (a+b)^24×21​×a×b+c2=(a+b)2

The notes stop there. Completing it: 2ab+c2=a2+2ab+b22ab+c^2=a^2+2ab+b^22ab+c2=a2+2ab+b2, so c2=a2+b2c^2=a^2+b^2c2=a2+b2.

This is the clearest case of the notes' habit: set up the decisive line, leave the arithmetic. Two of the four diagrams are drawn and then abandoned before any algebra, which is a deliberate revision prompt rather than an omission.

Method fingerprint

  • Area dissection of a square, edges labelled on all four sides.
  • Regions carry their areas where algebra is done (a2a^2a2, ababab, ababab, b2b^2b2) and bare letters A/B/C/D where it is not.
  • Pythagoras via four triangles in an (a+b)(a+b)(a+b) square: 4×12ab+c2=(a+b)24\times\tfrac12 ab + c^2 = (a+b)^24×21​ab+c2=(a+b)2.
  • Two of the four diagrams have no algebra written at all — (a−b)2(a-b)^2(a−b)2 and a2−b2a^2-b^2a2−b2.
