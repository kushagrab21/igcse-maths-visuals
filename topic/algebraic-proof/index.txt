<p><strong>Two letters matter.</strong> Scenario 2 uses <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>n</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">n</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.4306em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">n</span></span></span></span> and <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>k</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">k</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0315em;">k</span></span></span></span> because the two odd numbers are
independent; using <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>n</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">n</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.4306em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">n</span></span></span></span> twice would only prove it for the same number. The notes make this
change silently — no note explains it.</p>
<p><strong>Corrections the notes make to themselves.</strong> &quot;positive&quot; is caret-inserted in Scenario 1 and
&quot;odd&quot; in Scenario 2. Both are corrections to the <em>precision of the statement</em>, not the
algebra — the notes treat stating the claim exactly as part of the proof.</p>

<h2>Method fingerprint</h2>
<ul>
<li>Fixed opening: &#39;Let 2 consecutive odd numbers be 2n+1 and 2n+3&#39;.</li>
<li>Odd as <strong>2n+1</strong>, never 2n−1; consecutive odds by +2, not a second letter.</li>
<li>Two <em>independent</em> odd numbers use <strong>two letters</strong>: 2n+1 and 2k+1.</li>
<li>Proof terminates by reaching a <strong>form</strong> — 2(…), 2(…)+1, 8(…) — with <strong>no concluding sentence</strong>.</li>
<li>Precision corrections inserted by caret: &#39;positive&#39;, &#39;odd&#39;.</li>
<li>Place-value: two-digit number as 10x+y, reverse 10y+x, kept in place-value form, then the <strong>&#39;borrow 10&#39;</strong> regrouping.</li>
